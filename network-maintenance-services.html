Network Maintenance Services & AMC in UAE

Linkcore Information Technology provides comprehensive **Network Maintenance Services** and **IT Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)** for businesses across the UAE. We understand that network downtime equates to lost revenue, which is why our proactive support ensures your IT infrastructure remains available, secure, and efficient.

Our maintenance solutions cover everything from routine firmware updates and performance monitoring to emergency troubleshooting for routers, switches, servers, and cabling systems. Serving Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and Sharjah, we act as your dedicated IT department.

Why Your Business Needs Professional Network Maintenance

Modern businesses rely heavily on digital infrastructure. A neglected network can lead to slow speeds, security breaches, and catastrophic hardware failures. Our preventative maintenance approach identifies potential issues before they disrupt your operations.

Proactive Monitoring

We monitor your network health, identifying bottlenecks and hardware degradation early to prevent unscheduled outages.

Emergency Response

When critical issues arise, our team provides rapid remote and on-site support to get your business back online fast.

Our Network Maintenance Services

What our IT AMC Covers:

  • Firmware Updates – Keeping routers and firewalls patched against vulnerabilities
  • Network Security Audits – Regular checks for unauthorized access points
  • Hardware Health Checks – Monitoring temperature, CPU load, and port status
  • Server Maintenance – Updates, backups, and error log reviews
  • Cable Management – Organizing messy rack cabinets (re-patching & labeling)
  • Wi-Fi Optimization – Adjusting channels and power for best coverage
  • Data Backup Verification – Ensuring your critical data is recoverable
  • User Support – Assisting staff with connectivity issues
