Frequently Asked Questions

What areas in UAE do you cover for network installation?

We provide comprehensive network cabling and IT infrastructure services across all Emirates, including Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, and Ras Al Khaimah. Our team can mobilize quickly for projects in Free Zones and mainland locations.

Do you provide warranty on cabling and devices?

Yes, absolutely. We offer a standard warranty on all installation workmanship. Additionally, all hardware (routers, switches, cabinets) comes with the original manufacturer's warranty (ranging from 1 to 3 years depending on the brand like Cisco, D-Link, or Ubiquiti).

Can you help with moving our office IT to a new location?

Yes, we specialize in Office IT Relocation. We handle the dismantling of server racks, safe transport of equipment, and re-installation at your new office. We also set up the new structured cabling and Wi-Fi to ensure you are operational on Day 1.

What is the difference between Cat6 and Cat6a cabling?

Cat6 supports speeds up to 10Gbps for short distances (up to 55 meters), while Cat6a supports 10Gbps up to 100 meters and has better shielding against interference. For modern offices in Dubai looking to future-proof their network, we generally recommend Cat6a.
